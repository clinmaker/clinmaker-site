Ready to demo

Two modules you can see working today.

Both work on one structured study, from the protocol to the casebook. They are available for demos and pilots, and not yet in production use.

01Ready to demo

Protocol Digitization

Write, digitize or import a protocol in ICH M11 order (the harmonized protocol template) on USDM 4.0 (CDISC's standard model for study design).

02Ready to demo

Study Build

The casebook, built from the protocol with CDASH-aligned forms (CDISC's data collection standard) and exported as CDISC ODM (an open format for exchanging study designs).

Already in the platform

The audit trail, roles and administration, e-signatures and in-app help are built into the platform today, so they are not on the roadmap.

How the platform works →

In development

Ten modules, from data ingestion to submission.

The modules fall into three groups: collect and clean, review and lock, submit and measure. Each will read a copy of your data, record where it came from and never change the original. Your EDC (electronic data capture) and other source systems stay the system of record.

03Collect and clean

Data Ingestion

Reads copies of EDC, lab and other external data.

In development
04Collect and clean

Data Validation

Rule checks on the data, from single fields to checks across sources.

In development

Query Management

One query lifecycle across checks, reconciliation and coding.

In development
06Collect and clean

Medical Coding

In development
