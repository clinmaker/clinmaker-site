About

The clinical data stack, rethought as one platform.

ClinMaker is built by people who've spent careers running clinical trials — and who've felt every seam in the legacy stack.

Our thesis

Most clinical data work is plumbing, not science.

A typical Phase III trial uses eight or more tools: an EDC, a coding system, a SDTM mapper, a SAE reconciler, a query manager, an analytics layer, a CDISC standards library, and a workflow engine. Each has its own user list, its own audit trail, its own integration team. The teams spend more time exchanging files than analysing them.

The cost is not just budget — it's lock time, audit risk, and the inability to know, at any moment, how clean your data really is.

ClinMaker exists to collapse that stack into a coherent platform: one canonical data model, one audit trail, one role model, twelve cooperating agents. Every action is recorded. Every artefact is traceable. Every standard is enforced where data is collected — not patched in at submission.

Our principles

What we won't ship.

A short list of things we hold ourselves to. These aren't aspirational — they're hard constraints on what reaches our customers.

No unaudited mutation

If a value changed, the audit trail explains who, what, when, and why. There are no exceptions.

No black-box AI

Every AI decision surfaces its inputs, its confidence, and its rationale. Humans approve or reject; the system records the decision.

No standards as afterthought

CDISC, USDM, MedDRA, WHODrug are wired into the data model — not bolted on at export.

No deferred compliance

GxP isn't an enterprise tier you upgrade to. It's the floor for every customer, every study, every environment.

No vendor lock-in

Export your data, your audit trail, your mapping specs at any time. Open formats, open standards.

No fake "AI-powered"

If a workflow could run with deterministic logic, that's what we ship. AI is for problems that genuinely need judgement — not as decoration.
