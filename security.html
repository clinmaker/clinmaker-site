Security & Compliance

GxP-grade by design, not by retrofit.

Audit trail, RBAC, MFA, separation of duties, and encryption are foundational architecture — not features you toggle on later. Inspection-readiness is the default state.

Audit trail

Append-only. ALCOA+. 7-year retention.

Every GxP-relevant action is recorded as an immutable audit entry. Tamper-evident storage. Searchable by study, subject, user, record type, event type, and date range. Exportable as PDF or machine-readable JSON for inspection.

Every action audited

Create, update, delete, approve, sign — actor, timestamp, previous/new values, reason, study/site/subject context. AI actions distinguishable from human actions.

ALCOA+ aligned

Attributable, Legible, Contemporaneous, Original, Accurate — plus Complete, Consistent, Enduring, Available. The 9 principles map to specific audit columns.

Retention & immutability

At least as long as the linked clinical records. Write-once from the application layer; tamper detection at the storage layer.

Access control

RBAC. MFA. Separation of duties.

Unique user accounts — no shared logins, ever. Eleven predefined roles with fine-grained permissions per module and per data domain. Conflicting roles cannot stack on a single user.

Eleven predefined roles

System Admin, Platform Owner, QA/Compliance, Data Standards Lead, Study DM, Safety Physician, Medical Coder, CRA/Monitor, Site User, Biostat/Programmer, Study Viewer. Extendable per sponsor.

MFA & OTP everywhere

Password + OTP, or OTP-only where policy permits. MFA enforced for privileged roles. Channels: email, SMS, authenticator app. OTPs are random, single-use, time-limited, rate-limited.

Separation of duties

The user who creates a record cannot approve it. The user who codes cannot QC their own coding. Conflict-of-interest patterns are blocked at the permission layer.

Onboarding & review

Account creation gated on training completion + approval. Role changes audited with effective dates and reasons. Periodic access reviews. Immediate offboarding. Bulk operations preserve individual auditability.

Infrastructure

Encrypted in transit, at rest, and between modules.

Encryption

TLS 1.3 in transit. AES-256-GCM at rest. Database-level encryption keys rotated per the sponsor's policy. Backups encrypted independently.

AWS hosting, US region

EC2 + RDS Postgres + S3 + CloudFront. Private VPC. Bastion-only SSH. Container images scanned on every build.

Three environments

DEV / SANDBOX / PROD with separate databases, separate keys, separate logging. Code promotion is tag-gated. Production deploys are user-authorised.

Per-row blinding

Blinding scope enforced at the repository layer — not just the UI. Treatment-arm fields hidden from blinded roles in every API response. Optional segregated-storage mode for sponsors who require physical separation.

GDPR pseudonymization

Subject-PII erasure workflow with dual sign-off (DPO + QA). Deterministic hashing replaces PII; clinical data is preserved per GxP retention rules.

Monitoring & observability

Structured logs, request tracing, real-time error alerts. Production diagnostics workflow for on-call without SSH.

Validation evidence

Inspection-ready out of the box.

Validation deliverables are generated from the same source of truth as the code: User Requirements Specification, Functional Requirements Specification, Validation Plan, Risk Assessment, IQ/OQ/PQ scripts, Traceability Matrix. Refreshed on every release.
