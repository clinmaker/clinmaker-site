What you get

One source of truth for every team that touches the study.

Today each team rebuilds the protocol in its own tool, and every amendment starts that over. With ClinMaker, writers, data managers and study leads all work from the same structured study.

Clinical and medical writing

A protocol that is structured the moment it is written.

Author in ICH M11 order with guidance on every section. Objectives, eligibility and design are held as data, and ClinMaker flags when the text and the design fall out of step.

Data management

A build that starts from the approved protocol.

Create the casebook from the structured study, with CDASH forms and SDTM targets in place, and export it to your EDC. When the protocol changes, the build shows it is out of date.

Study leadership

See what an amendment touches before you sign it.

Each change runs against 64 impact rules across IRB, sites, the build, documents and vendors. Each finding cites the rule behind it, and the team signs off once.
