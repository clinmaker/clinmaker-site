Questions sponsors ask us.

How ClinMaker fits with your EDC and your teams, what your QA and IT reviewers will want to know, and what it is like to work with us.

ClinMaker and your systems

My EDC vendor already builds studies from the protocol. Why ClinMaker?

If you run one EDC and only need the build, your EDC vendor's tools may be enough. ClinMaker is for sponsors and CROs that want the protocol and the casebook on one structured study, run more than one EDC, or need to handle amendments once, before they reach the casebook. The casebook exports as CDISC ODM 1.3.2, Medidata Rave ALS or Veeva Vault SDS, and your EDC stays the system your sites use.

Why not have our CRO build the casebook?

Your CRO can keep doing the work, in ClinMaker. The casebook is built from the approved protocol instead of being re-keyed from a document, and an amendment is assessed once and carried into a new draft of the build. Your CRO works in the same study as you.

Can we use Protocol Digitization without Study Build?

Yes. Each module runs on the same platform. Study Build can also start from an uploaded protocol or a USDM JSON file. The structured study is yours and exports as USDM JSON.

Quality, security and AI

Is ClinMaker validated?

Not yet. The platform is designed for 21 CFR Part 11, EU Annex 11 and ICH E6(R3) controls, but no IQ/OQ/PQ has been executed. Validation documentation is in preparation, and we share where it stands with every early adopter. The sponsor or CRO that uses the system validates it for its intended use, and we support that work.

How do electronic signatures work?

The signer re-enters their password and gives a reason. The signature is linked to the record it signs, such as an approved protocol, an amendment impact assessment or an export override.

Do you train AI on our data?

[to confirm: AI provider data terms] A patient-identifier filter checks every prompt before it reaches a model, and AI drafts, extractions and decisions are written to the audit trail. A person decides on every suggestion.

Can we export everything?

Yes. The study exports as USDM JSON once it passes the export checks, or with a signed override. The casebook exports as CDISC ODM 1.3.2 XML, with codelists from CDISC Controlled Terminology where one exists.

Working with us

You're a new company with no customers yet. Why take the risk?

Don't bet a live study on us. Start with a 30-minute live demo. As an early adopter, you then run ClinMaker alongside a study you've already built, so nothing live depends on it.

What does the early adopter program involve?

One study you've already built, under mutual NDA. Your protocol is structured and a casebook is built from it in ClinMaker, next to your existing build, and you compare the two with your team. See how it works.

How quickly do you reply?

Within two business days. Every message reaches the founders.

Still have a question?

Ask the people building ClinMaker.

Write to contact@clinmaker.com, or ask it in a 30-minute live demo. We reply within two business days.
