Platform overview

Write the study once. Every team builds from it.

Today, a protocol is a document that each later team re-keys: the casebook, the documents, the submission. ClinMaker keeps the protocol as one structured study and builds the rest from it, on open standards, with a record of every decision.

01

One structured study

The protocol is held as data on USDM 4.0, in ICH M11 order, so nothing downstream starts from a retyped copy.

02

Built on open standards

CDISC throughout: CDASH forms, controlled terminology, ODM and USDM exports you can take anywhere.

03

People decide

AI drafts, people approve. Reviews, approvals and e-signatures are recorded in an append-only audit trail.

04

Works with your EDC

The casebook exports to the EDC you already run. Your systems stay the source of truth.

Standards

Open CDISC standards you can export.

USDM 4.0 is CDISC's model for study design, ICH M11 the harmonized protocol template, CDASH the standard for collecting data and CDISC ODM an open format for study designs. Before the protocol is exported as USDM JSON, a gate runs CDISC CORE conformance checks, and overriding an error takes an e-signature and a reason.

How AI is used

AI proposes. A person decides. The system remembers.

  1. 01

    Propose AI

    AI drafts sections and extracts structure from uploaded files. Every draft is marked Advisory.

  2. 02

    Decide Person

    Nothing is applied automatically. A person accepts, edits or rejects each proposal.

  3. 03

    Remember System

    AI drafts, extractions and decisions are written to the audit trail.
