The rules

Five rules, so there is nothing to ask.

  1. 01

    Phase

    A protocol is priced at the highest phase on its title page, so a Phase II/III protocol uses the late rate.

  2. 02

    One protocol

    One protocol number, with every amendment, site, country and user, until the study closes.

  3. 03

    Adding a module

    A module added later costs its weight times the protocol's rate, from when it starts.

  4. 04

    Payment

    Each module is invoiced in two halves: when it starts on a protocol, and 90 days later.

  5. 05

    Price lock

    A protocol's rate never changes during its life.
