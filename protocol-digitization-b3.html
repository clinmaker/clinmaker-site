Write the protocol as a structured study.

Every team after the protocol re-keys it, and every amendment starts that over. In Protocol Digitization, your writers create the protocol once, in ICH M11 order, as one structured study on USDM 4.0 that the next team reads directly.

Structure your own protocol →

Protocol workspace

From a document to a structured, approved protocol.

Five steps, in the product.

  1. 01

    Start from what you have

    Upload a PDF, Word or text file, or a USDM 4.0 JSON, and AI extracts the study for you to review.

  2. 02

    Follow the ICH M11 guide

    Guided steps take the team from study identification onward, showing what is next and what is still open.

  3. 03

    AI proposes, you decide

    Improve wording, apply ICH M11 tone or check against the schedule; every change arrives as a proposal.

  4. 04

    Review section by section

    Each section moves from Draft to In review to Reviewed, with its reviewers assigned.

  5. 05

    Export open formats

    Export the same study as USDM 4.0, ClinicalTrials.gov XML and EU CTIS JSON.

Amendments, assessed once

Follow one change all the way through.

A typical Phase III protocol has 3.4 substantial amendments.1 Here is one of them in ClinMaker: a 12-lead ECG added at Week 4.

01Change one sentenceStart an amendment of the approved protocol. It becomes the next draft, and only what you change is reviewed.
Sample protocolv1 · Approvedv1.1 · Draft

§9.2 · Safety assessments

Safety Assessments

  • Vital signs (blood pressure, heart rate, respiratory rate, temperature)
  • 12-lead ECG at Screening, Baseline, Week 4, Week 12, with central reading for QTcF
  • Clinical laboratory panel (hematology, chemistry, urinalysis) at Screening, Baseline, Week 4, Week 12

1 changeEvery other section carries forward as approved.
