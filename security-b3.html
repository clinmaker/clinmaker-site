Controls

The controls, as they work today.

Each one is part of the platform that Protocol Digitization and Study Build run on, mapped to the clauses your SOPs already cite.

Audit trail

Append-only and hash-chained. Each record names the user and the time, and a chain verifier reports any break. Database rules block edits and deletes.

21 CFR 11.10(e) · Annex 11 §9

Electronic signatures

The signer re-enters their password and gives a reason, and the signature is linked to the record it signs: a protocol approval, an impact assessment or an export override.

21 CFR 11.50 · 11.70 · 11.200 · Annex 11 §14

Roles and blinding

Fine-grained sponsor roles with separation of duties, and blinding rules that limit who can see unblinded information.

21 CFR 11.10(d) · 11.10(g) · Annex 11 §12

Single sign-on

Sign in through your identity provider with OIDC or SAML, with SCIM user provisioning, on the Enterprise plan.

21 CFR 11.10(d) · 11.300 · Annex 11 §12

Tenant isolation

Each sponsor's data is kept apart at the database level, using row-level security.

21 CFR 11.10(d) · Annex 11 §7

Checks before export

Before a protocol is exported as USDM JSON, a gate runs schema, completeness and CDISC CORE conformance checks. Overriding an error takes an e-signature and a reason.

21 CFR 11.10(f) · Annex 11 §5

“Designed for” means the control is part of how ClinMaker is built. It is not a certification, and it does not replace the validation you carry out for your own intended use.

AI, governed

A person decides on every AI suggestion.

  • AI output is marked Advisory, and its accuracy is not yet measured.
  • A person accepts, edits or rejects each suggestion. No build is approved without a person.
  • Sections imported from a PDF or Word file keep the text they came from.
  • Every prompt is checked for patient identifiers, such as subject IDs, before it reaches a model.
  • AI drafts, extractions and decisions are written to the audit trail.

Not every feature uses AI. Building the casebook from the protocol uses none.
