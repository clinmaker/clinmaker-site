Write the protocol as a structured study.

Every team after the protocol re-keys it, and every amendment starts that over. In Protocol Digitization, your writers create the protocol once, in ICH M11 order, as one structured study on USDM 4.0 that the next team reads directly.

How AI is used here

AI writes first drafts, and a person reviews each one.

AI drafts sections, objectives, endpoints, eligibility and the schedule, and extracts structure from a PDF or Word file. Not every feature uses it.

How AI is governed →
01

Imports keep their source

Each section imported from a PDF or Word file keeps the text it came from.

02

Marked Advisory

AI output is labeled Advisory. Its accuracy is not yet measured, so a person checks every draft.

03

Never applied on its own

A person accepts, edits or rejects each draft. Sections accepted from AI are stamped as AI-accepted.

04

Prompts checked, decisions recorded

A patient-identifier filter checks every prompt before it reaches a model. The audit trail records AI drafts, extractions and decisions.
