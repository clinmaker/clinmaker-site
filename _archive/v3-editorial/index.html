A coherent platform replaces a stack of point tools — and the swivel-chair, the email handoff, and the spreadsheet reconciliation that came with them.

100% audit-trail coverage on every GxP-relevant action. USDM 4.0 round-trip scoring ~100/100. 30+ platform modules, 500+ API endpoints, 15 vendor adapters, 11 RBAC roles. 0 unaudited mutations, by design.

Who runs it

Built for the people running real trials.

Data managers exhausted by reconciling between five vendors. Biostatisticians who want a clean SDTM package by lock day, not three weeks after. QA teams who'd like the audit trail to actually answer auditors' questions. Sponsors who've watched study timelines slip by a quarter because the coding queue stalled in a separate tool.

ClinMaker collapses that work into one platform with one model. Twelve agents do the routine work autonomously and surface a human decision only when the situation actually requires judgement. Every decision — human or AI — is recorded.
