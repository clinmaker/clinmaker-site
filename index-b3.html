Why it starts with the protocol

Where it starts

The protocol is still a Word document, so every later team re-keys it.

Authoring tools stop at the document. EDC build tools start from a finished one. Nothing connects the two, and every amendment starts the cycle over. So ClinMaker starts where the re-keying starts: the protocol, held as one structured study.

Amendments, assessed once

Follow one change all the way through.

A typical Phase III protocol has 3.4 substantial amendments.2 Here is one of them in ClinMaker: a 12-lead ECG added at Week 4.

01Change one sentenceStart an amendment of the approved protocol. It becomes the next draft, and only what you change is reviewed.
Sample protocolv1 · Approvedv1.1 · Draft

§9.2 · Safety assessments

Safety Assessments

  • Vital signs (blood pressure, heart rate, respiratory rate, temperature)
  • 12-lead ECG at Screening, Baseline, Week 4, Week 12, with central reading for QTcF
  • Clinical laboratory panel (hematology, chemistry, urinalysis) at Screening, Baseline, Week 4, Week 12

1 changeEvery other section carries forward as approved.

02Classify itAdministrative, Minor or Major, with a written rationale. Regulatory Affairs signs off on the tier.
