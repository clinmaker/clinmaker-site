Why we started

A note from the founders.

SRMC

50+ years combined · CROs, biotech, large pharma

We spent our careers on the receiving end of EDC (electronic data capture) builds: more than 50 years between us in data management, statistical programming and clinical operations, at CROs, biotech and large pharma.

Every hand-off meant building the same study again, and the numbers match what we saw: 68 days to build and release a study database,1 3.4 substantial amendments in a typical Phase III protocol,2 and up to $1 million in unplanned direct cost for each one.3

So we built ClinMaker around one structured study, from the protocol to the casebook. Two modules are ready to demo today. We'd rather show you two working things than promise twelve.

Why now

The standards for structuring a protocol now exist.

Until recently, protocols had no shared structure, so they could only be documents. CDISC's USDM (Unified Study Definitions Model) now gives study design a shared data model. ICH M11 gives the protocol a harmonized template and section order. CDASH and CDISC Controlled Terminology already define how casebook data is collected.

Together they make a structured study possible, and a casebook built from it. ClinMaker is built on these open standards, so your study leaves in open formats.

Mission and vision

What we're here to do, and where it leads.

Mission

We make the work behind clinical trials simpler, faster and worthy of trust, so the people who run trials can focus on the science, and the answers patients are waiting for come sooner.

In short: Simplify the work behind clinical trials.

Vision

To become the platform clinical research teams trust with their most important work, from a study's first draft to its final submission, in a world where no treatment is delayed because the work behind it was too slow.

In short: Become the most trusted name in clinical trial work.

Five values we hold ourselves to

Earn trust, then prove it.

Everything we do can be shown, explained and checked, by a customer, a regulator or each other.

People decide.

Technology drafts, sorts and suggests; a person makes every decision that matters, and stands behind it.

Say only what's true.

We claim what's real today and are open about what isn't yet. We'd rather show two working things than promise twelve.

Do it right from the start.

Quality is how we work from the first line, not a check at the end.

Remember who it's for.

Behind every record is a person who volunteered for a trial, and a patient waiting for its answer.
