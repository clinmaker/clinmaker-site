Frequently asked

Common questions on pricing & terms.

How is pricing calculated?
Three drivers: number of concurrent studies, total subject volume across the program, and number of vendor integrations (EDC, labs, imaging, ePRO, RTSM, device feeds, etc.). We don't charge per user, per query, or per audit row.
What's included in the platform versus add-on?
Everything described on the Platform page is included in every tier — protocol authoring, study build, ingestion, validation, query mgmt, coding, reconciliation, SDTM/ADaM, analytics, audit, user admin, help center. No feature is locked behind a higher tier.
Do you offer a sandbox / UAT environment?
Yes. Every contract includes a dedicated SANDBOX environment alongside production. SANDBOX runs the same code with isolated data — used for test loads, integration validation, and training.
Who owns the data?
You do. Always. Sponsor data is contractually and technically segregated. Full export is available at any time in open formats (CSV, JSON, SDTM datasets, Define.xml, audit trail).
What about validation evidence?
A complete URS Pack (User Requirements Specification, Functional Requirements Specification, Validation Plan, Risk Assessment, IQ/OQ/PQ scripts, Traceability Matrix) is provided as part of every contract. Refreshed on every release.
Implementation timeline?
A first study typically goes live within 6–10 weeks from contract signature: 2 weeks SANDBOX onboarding, 2–4 weeks study build, 2 weeks UAT, 1 week go-live. Subsequent studies in the same program are faster.
Can we extend with our own libraries?
Yes. Sponsor edit-check libraries, SDTM extension domains, sponsor-specific codelists, and authoring templates all layer on top. Your standards inherit from CDISC — they don't replace them.
Do you support SSO?
Sponsor and Enterprise tiers include SAML 2.0 / OIDC SSO with your IdP (Okta, Azure AD, Ping). Pilot uses platform-managed accounts with MFA.
