Every protocol becomes a document first, and data much later.

Authoring tools stop at the document. EDC build tools begin from a finished one. Nothing connects the two, so each amendment reopens the build.

ClinMaker begins with the protocol itself, held as one structured study.

Your challenge

ClinMaker delivers

01

Protocol to database. Teams retype the approved protocol into the database by hand, and the build takes over two months before first patient.

Structured from the start. The protocol is held as data, and Study Build turns it into CDASH forms, codelists and edit checks, exported as CDISC ODM for your EDC.

02

Every amendment reopens the build. Each change means another rebuild, another change order and weeks of rework across teams.

Change once. An amendment is classified once, traced to every step it affects and signed by a reviewer before a new draft of the build opens.

03

Disconnected handoffs. Writing, data management, statistics and regulatory each keep their own copy, so every handoff adds delay and risk.

Built on open standards. ICH M11, USDM 4.0 and CDISC throughout, so every team works from the same study without re-keying.
