Privacy policy

How ClinMaker, LLC handles the information you share with us through this website.

Who we are

ClinMaker, LLC (“ClinMaker”, “we”, “us”) runs clinmaker.com and decides how the personal information collected through it is used. This policy covers this website and its forms. It does not cover the ClinMaker product at app.clinmaker.com, which is governed by separate agreements.

For anything in this policy, write to contact@clinmaker.com, or to ClinMaker, LLC, [to confirm: postal address].

What we collect

When you send the request form, we collect:

  • your name, work email and company, which are required;
  • if you choose to add them, your role, organization type, study phases, studies per year, the EDC you use, your timeline and your message;
  • what you asked for (a demo, the early adopter program or a quote), and whether you ticked product news.

When you email us, we receive what you send.

When you visit, our website host records technical information, such as your IP address and browser, to deliver the site and keep it secure. [to confirm: GitHub Pages logging] We don't use cookies, advertising tags or tracking pixels.

Please don't send patient data, protocols or other confidential study information through a website form or by email. For the early adopter program, we sign a mutual NDA first and then send a secure upload link.

Why we use it, and our legal basis

  • To reply to your request and arrange a demo, the early adopter program or a quote. We rely on our legitimate interest in answering people who contact us, and on taking the steps you asked for before a possible contract.
  • To send product news, only if you ticked the box. We rely on your consent, which you can withdraw at any time by unsubscribing or by emailing us.
  • To keep the site and our records secure, and to meet our legal obligations. We rely on our legitimate interests and on legal obligation.

We don't sell your personal information or share it for advertising, and we don't use it to make automated decisions about you.

Who we share it with

A small number of service providers process information on our behalf, under contract, and only to provide their service:

  • Formspree, our form service, which receives form submissions and passes them to us. [to confirm: where Formspree stores data, and our data processing agreement with it]
  • Our email provider, which hosts contact@clinmaker.com. [to confirm: provider name]
  • GitHub, which hosts this website on GitHub Pages.
  • An email newsletter service, used only for people who ticked product news. [to confirm: provider name, once chosen]

We may also share information with our professional advisers, or where the law requires it.

Transfers outside your country

ClinMaker is based in the United States, and our service providers may process information in the United States or in other countries. When personal information is transferred from the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, we rely on a recognised safeguard: the EU–US Data Privacy Framework and its UK and Swiss extensions where a provider is certified, or standard contractual clauses. [to confirm: the safeguard for each provider]

How long we keep it

  • Requests that don't lead to working together are deleted 180 days after our last reply.
  • If we go on to work together, we keep the information for as long as the relationship lasts, and afterwards for as long as the law requires. [to confirm]
  • Product news: we keep your name and email until you unsubscribe. We then keep a record that you unsubscribed, so that we don't contact you again.

How we protect it

The site and the form are served over HTTPS. Only the people who answer requests can see submissions, and our form and email accounts use multi-factor sign-in. [to confirm] If a breach affects your information, we'll tell you and the authorities as the law requires.

Your rights

Wherever you live, you can ask us to:

  • tell you what personal information we hold about you, and give you a copy;
  • correct it, or delete it;
  • stop or limit how we use it, or object to it;
  • stop sending you product news. Every product news email has an unsubscribe link.

Email contact@clinmaker.com. We reply within one month, and we may ask you to confirm your identity first. We won't treat you differently for using your rights. You can also complain to your local data protection authority.

Where you live

European Economic Area, United Kingdom and Switzerland. The legal bases above apply. You also have the right to receive your information in a portable format, and to withdraw consent at any time without affecting what we did before. California and other US states. In the past 12 months we have collected identifiers (your name and email) and professional information (your company, role and study details), from you, for the purposes above. We don't sell or share personal information, and we don't collect sensitive personal information. You may use an authorized agent to make a request on your behalf. [to confirm]

Canada. We send product news only to people who ticked the box to receive it.

Elsewhere, including Brazil, India, Singapore, Australia and Japan: you have the rights your local law gives you, and you can use them in the same way.

Children

This website is for professionals in clinical research. It is not meant for children, and we don't knowingly collect their information.

Cookies and similar technologies

This site doesn't set cookies. Its fonts are served from this site, so pages don't contact a font service. [to confirm]

The site uses your browser's session storage to remember, during one visit, that you've already seen a notice. It stays in your browser, isn't sent to us, and is cleared when you close the tab.

If we add website analytics, we'll choose a service that doesn't use cookies or identify you, and we'll name it here. [to confirm: analytics service, if added]

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top of this page. If a change matters to people on our product news list, we'll tell them by email.
