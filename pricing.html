§02 / 03 topic: faq common questions

Common questions.

How is pricing calculated? +

Three drivers: number of concurrent studies, total subject volume across the program, and number of vendor integrations. We don't charge per user, per query, or per audit row. Quote within 24 hours of the wizard submission.

What's included versus add-on? +

The two prototype modules (Protocol Authoring + Study Build) are included in every tier with the full validation pack, RBAC, MFA, and sandbox environment. No feature gating, no compliance gating. Roadmap modules join under the same contract as they ship; early-access seats negotiable per tier.

Do you offer a sandbox / UAT environment? +

Yes. Every contract includes a dedicated SANDBOX environment alongside production. SANDBOX runs the same code with isolated data — used for test loads, integration validation, and training.

Who owns the data? +

You do. Always. Sponsor data is contractually and technically segregated. Full export is available at any time in open formats — CSV, JSON, SDTM datasets, Define.xml, audit trail.

What about validation evidence? +

A complete URS Pack (User Requirements, Functional Requirements, Validation Plan, Risk Assessment, IQ/OQ/PQ scripts, Traceability Matrix) is provided as part of every contract. Refreshed on every release.

Implementation timeline? +

First study goes live within 6–10 weeks from contract signature: 2 weeks SANDBOX onboarding, 2–4 weeks study build, 2 weeks UAT, 1 week go-live. Subsequent studies in the same program are faster.

Can we extend with our own libraries? +

Yes. Sponsor edit-check libraries, SDTM extension domains, sponsor codelists, and authoring templates all layer on top of CDISC. Your standards inherit from theirs.

Do you support SSO? +

Sponsor and Enterprise tiers include SAML 2.0 / OIDC SSO with your IdP (Okta, Azure AD, Ping). Pilot uses platform-managed accounts with MFA.
