How we work

Five values we work by.

We wrote them down early, while the company is small. They shape what we build, what we say about it, and who we hire.

Read our values in full →
  • Earn trust, then prove it. Our work can be shown, explained and checked.
  • People decide. Technology drafts and suggests. A person makes every decision that matters.
  • Say only what's true. We claim what's real today and are open about what isn't yet.
  • Do it right from the start. Quality is part of the work from the first line.
  • Remember who it's for. Behind every record is a trial volunteer, and a patient waiting for the answer.
