Every team works from the same structured study.

Medical writing, data management, regulatory, quality and clinical operations each start from one protocol, held as structured data. Here is what each team does with it today.

Core use cases

Three modules, one study.

The Protocol workspace and Study Build are ready to demo. The Document Center turns the same study into the documents each team needs.

PReady to demo

Protocol workspace

  • Write a protocol in ICH M11From structured study data, section by section.
  • Import the protocol you havePDF, Word or USDM JSON, reviewed before it is applied.
  • Draft with AI, decide as a teamSuggestions are Advisory until a person accepts them.
  • Approve with an e-signatureAfter review, comments and a completeness check.
  • Export and amendUSDM, registry drafts and PDF; amendments assessed once.

Study Build

  • Build the casebook from the protocolFour ways to start, all from the same study.
  • Use standard CDASH formsForms and codelists come from the library.
  • Generate edit checks by ruleReviewed with the forms, no AI.
  • Run UAT and sign it offEach stage snapshotted, approval e-signed.
  • Export to your EDCRave ALS, Vault SDS or CDISC ODM.

Document Center

  • Generate study documentsSynopsis, data management plan, SAP shell, transfer specs and UAT scripts, from the study.
  • Review and signDraft, in review, approved and effective, with an approver other than the author.
  • Know when a document is out of dateIt is flagged when the study it came from changes.
  • Keep it in one placeRegistry exports, approval packages and impact reports, governed together.
