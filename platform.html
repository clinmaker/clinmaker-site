The platform

What both modules stand on.

One structured study

Stored in USDM 4.0, CDISC's model for study design. Every module reads from it and writes to it.

CDISC standards library

Controlled Terminology, CDASHIG and SDTMIG, built in.

Append-only audit trail

Hash-linked records, with a verifier that reports breaks and gaps. Database rules block edits and deletes.

E-signatures

The signer re-enters their password and gives a reason, and the signature is linked to the record it signs.

Roles and permissions

Fine-grained sponsor roles, separation of duties and blinding rules.

Single sign-on, MFA and SCIM

OIDC or SAML sign-in, multi-factor authentication and user provisioning.

Tenant isolation

Each customer's data kept apart at the database level.

AI governance

Model provider and routing settings, an AI evidence policy, and the Advisory label on every AI output.

Protocol library

Search and compare protocols, linked by therapeutic area and intervention, and reuse objectives, endpoints and eligibility.

Standards

Open CDISC standards you can export.

USDM 4.0 is CDISC's model for study design, ICH M11 the harmonized protocol template, CDASH the standard for collecting data and CDISC ODM an open format for study designs. Before the protocol is exported as USDM JSON, a gate runs CDISC CORE conformance checks, and overriding an error takes an e-signature and a reason.

How AI is used

AI proposes. A person decides. The system remembers.

  1. 01

    Propose AI

    AI drafts sections and extracts structure from uploaded files. Every draft is marked Advisory.

  2. 02

    Decide Person

    Nothing is applied automatically. A person accepts, edits or rejects each proposal.

  3. 03

    Remember System

    AI drafts, extractions and decisions are written to the audit trail.

Questions

ClinMaker and the systems you run today.

My EDC vendor already builds studies from the protocol. Why ClinMaker?

If you run one EDC and only need the build, your EDC vendor's tools may be enough. ClinMaker is for sponsors and CROs that want the protocol and the casebook on one structured study, run more than one EDC, or need to handle amendments once, before they reach the casebook.

ClinMaker isn't tied to one EDC. The casebook exports as CDISC ODM 1.3.2 XML, plus EDC-specific loader files [to confirm: which EDC loaders to name]. Your EDC stays the system your sites use and the source of the clinical data.

You're a new company with no customers yet. Why take the risk?

Don't bet a live study on us. Start with a 30-minute live demo. A pilot then runs alongside a study you've already built, so nothing live depends on it.

Why not have our CRO build the casebook?

Your CRO can keep doing the work, in ClinMaker. The casebook is built from the approved protocol instead of being re-keyed from a document, and an amendment is assessed once and carried into a new draft of the build. Your CRO works in the same study as you.

Can we use Protocol Digitization without Study Build?

Yes. Each module runs on the same platform. Study Build can also start from an uploaded protocol or a USDM JSON file. The structured study is yours and exports as USDM JSON.

Is ClinMaker validated?

Not yet. The platform is designed for 21 CFR Part 11, EU Annex 11 and ICH E6(R3) controls, but it has not been validated and no IQ/OQ/PQ has been executed. Validation documentation is being prepared; we share where it stands with every pilot sponsor.

The platform holds no real patient data. See Trust & compliance for the detail.
