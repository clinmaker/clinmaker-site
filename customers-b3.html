Built for the teams that turn a protocol into a study.

ClinMaker is for sponsors and CROs that want the protocol and the casebook on one structured study, and for the people inside them who do that work today. We're early, so you won't find logos here. This is who we built it for.

Who we serve

Three kinds of organization, one structured study.

Whoever builds your database, the protocol and the casebook stay on the same study, in open formats you own.

Emerging biotech

Small teams, early protocols and builds handed to a CRO. Keep the study in your hands, whoever builds the database.

  • Start from the Word protocol you have
  • Your CRO works in the same study
  • Open exports you own: USDM and CDISC ODM

Mid-size and large sponsors

Many studies, more than one EDC, and amendments across a portfolio. One structure for all of them.

  • Export to Rave, Vault or CDISC ODM
  • Each amendment assessed once and signed
  • Sponsor form templates, approved before use

CROs

Studies for many sponsors, each with its own standards and its own EDC. Build each one from its approved protocol.

  • Each sponsor's data kept apart
  • Builds start from the approved protocol
  • Export to the EDC each sponsor runs
