ClinMaker's controls today, and what isn't done yet.

ClinMaker is designed for 21 CFR Part 11, EU Annex 11 and ICH E6(R3) controls. These are the FDA and EU rules for electronic records and computerized systems, and the ICH good clinical practice guideline.

“Designed for” means the control is part of how ClinMaker is built. It is not a certification, and it does not replace the validation you carry out for your own intended use.

AI controls

A person decides on each AI suggestion, and the audit trail records the decision.

  • Source kept on import. Sections imported from a PDF or Word file keep the text they came from.
  • Marked Advisory. AI output carries an Advisory label. Its accuracy is not yet measured.
  • A person decides. A person accepts, edits or rejects each AI suggestion, and no build is approved without a person. Sections accepted from AI are stamped as AI-accepted.
  • A patient-identifier filter. Every prompt is checked for identifier patterns, such as subject IDs, before it reaches a model.
  • AI activity is audited. AI drafts, extractions and decisions are written to the audit trail.

FAQ

Questions QA and IT ask us.

For other questions, write to contact@clinmaker.com. We reply within two business days.

Is ClinMaker validated?

No. Validation documentation is in preparation and no IQ/OQ/PQ has been executed. The sponsor or CRO that uses the system validates it for its intended use, and we support that work.

How do electronic signatures work?

The signer re-enters their password and gives a reason. The signature is linked to the record it signs, such as an approved protocol, an amendment impact assessment or an export override.

What holds today: a patient-identifier filter checks every prompt before it reaches a model, and AI activity is written to the audit trail.

Can we export everything?

Yes. The study exports as USDM JSON once it passes the export checks, or with a signed override. The casebook exports as CDISC ODM 1.3.2 XML, with codelists from CDISC Controlled Terminology where one exists.
