Before you send

Questions about getting started.

For security, validation and data handling, see Trust & compliance.

Do we need to sign an NDA?

For a pilot, yes. We send a mutual NDA before any protocol is shared. A demo runs on our own demo study, so you don't need to share anything.

What do you need for a pilot?

One of your protocols, draft or final, Phase I to IV, as a PDF, a Word file or USDM JSON. The pilot runs alongside a study you've already built. Once the mutual NDA is signed, we send a secure upload link.

What does it cost?

There is no charge for a demo. For a pilot or ongoing use, ask for a quote and we'll price the modules you need and the studies you run.

What happens to our protocol afterward?

Only the people working on your pilot can open it, and we delete it on request. Retention period: [to confirm retention period]. More on how protocols are handled.
