What changes for your team

Less rework for writers. A clean handoff for everyone after them.

The protocol stops being a document each team re-reads and becomes the study itself, written, reviewed and amended in one place.

  1. Start fast

    Start from the protocol you already have.

    Import a Word, PDF or USDM file. AI extracts the study design, and your team reviews what it found, so nobody retypes a protocol that already exists.

    • Word, PDF, text or USDM 4.0 JSON
    • Objectives, eligibility and interventions extracted
    • A person reviews every extracted item

  2. Structured as you write

    ICH M11 order, with the structure built in.

    A guide takes the team section by section and shows what is still open. Key sections are bound to the structured study, and ClinMaker flags when the text and the design fall out of step.

    • All 84 ICH M11 sections, guided
    • 33 sections bound to the structured study
    • Section-by-section review and sign-off

  3. AI, governed

    AI drafts. Your experts decide.

    Improve wording, apply ICH M11 tone or check a section against the schedule. Every AI change arrives as a proposal, nothing is applied until a person accepts it, and every decision is recorded.

    • Changes arrive as proposals
    • Nothing applied without a person
    • AI drafts and decisions in the audit trail

  4. Amendments

    See everything an amendment touches, before you sign.

    A typical Phase III protocol has 3.4 substantial amendments.1 Classify each change, run it against impact rules for IRB, sites, the build, documents and vendors, and sign off once.

    • 64 impact rules, each citing its regulation
    • Only what changed is reviewed again
    • A new draft of the build opens; the live one stays put
