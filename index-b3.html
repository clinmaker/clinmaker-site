Where it starts

The protocol is still a Word document, so every later team re-keys it.

Authoring tools stop at the document. EDC build tools start from a finished one. Nothing connects the two, and every amendment starts the cycle over. So ClinMaker starts where the re-keying starts: the protocol, held as one structured study.

Your challenge

ClinMaker delivers

01

Protocol to database. Protocols are Word documents no system can read, so teams retype the study by hand, and building the database takes months before first patient.

Structured from the start. ClinMaker turns your protocol into structured data and builds the casebook into the Rave or Vault EDC you already run, ready to test.

02

Amendments and spend. Every protocol change means rebuilding the study, another change order and months of delay, with each Phase III amendment costing up to $1M.

Change once. Amendments update the study instead of restarting it, cutting rework for your team and change orders for your budget, so your runway lasts longer.

03

Control and confidence. When study scope shifts, the change has to travel across disconnected systems. Each handoff adds delay and risk, so it gets harder to keep timelines on track without compromising quality.

One connected workflow. Built on industry and sponsor standards, so every change moves through the study predictably. Your data, your way.
